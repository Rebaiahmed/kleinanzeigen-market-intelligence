# Market research reports

We plan to publish a market research report **every one to two weeks**, subject to having enough reliable, rights-cleared data and completing a privacy and methodology review. This is an editorial goal, not an automated publication schedule.

## Archive from September 2026

| Period | Status | Report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 2026 | No verified, rights-cleared dataset or report available yet | Not published |

The archive starts with September 2026 as a target for historical coverage; no report or observations from that period are currently available here. We will add a report only when its source, collection dates, permission, sample size and limitations can be documented. Synthetic examples in this repository are not market observations and will not be presented as historical research.

Each future report should distinguish asking prices from actual transaction prices, describe sample selection and limitations, and provide reproducible methods where publishing rights permit. See the [research methodology](../docs/methodology.md) and [dataset provenance](../datasets/README.md).
